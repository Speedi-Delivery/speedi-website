Privacy Policy.

How Speedi Delivery LLC handles information in the website, customer app, and driver app.

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This policy applies to the Speedi website, the Speedi customer app, and the Speedi Driver app (together, “Speedi”). It does not replace the terms, disclosures, or permissions presented in the apps or at checkout.

Information we collect

Customer app and website

  • Account and contact information, including name, email address, phone number, and account-verification information.
  • Delivery information, including saved addresses, delivery instructions, order contents, order history, tips, and support messages.
  • Payment and transaction information. Card details are entered through Stripe; Speedi does not intentionally store full payment-card numbers.
  • Device location only when you choose to grant permission for address selection or delivery-related features. We may retain the delivery address and associated coordinates you save or use for an order.
  • Push-notification device tokens and settings needed to send transactional notifications, such as order-status updates.
  • Website contact or work-application information, including the information you choose to submit through our forms.

Driver app

  • Driver account and contact information.
  • Order-assignment information needed to complete a delivery, including a customer's name, phone number, delivery address, instructions, and order details.
  • Delivery proof photos and, only for orders that require age verification, ID-verification records.
  • Push-notification device tokens and settings used for operational notifications, such as order assignments.

Information collected automatically

Our apps and services may process device, browser, network, session, diagnostic, and security information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and prevent misuse of Speedi. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts.

How we use information

  • Create and maintain accounts, process and deliver orders, provide support, and communicate about requested services.
  • Send account-verification, order, safety, and operational notifications. Where you choose to receive promotional messages, you may opt out through the message, device settings, or by contacting us.
  • Verify age when legally required, maintain delivery records, and protect customers, drivers, and Speedi.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve our website, apps, and services.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce the Terms of Service.

Service providers and disclosures

We use providers that process information on our behalf to run Speedi, including Stripe for payment processing, Firebase for push messaging, Google services for maps and geocoding, Twilio for text messaging, Cloudflare for website and file infrastructure, AWS-hosted application services, and FormSubmit for website-form delivery. These providers may use information only as needed to provide their services to Speedi.

We do not sell or rent personal information. Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in information, and SMS consent are not sold, rented, leased, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect people or services, or as part of a lawful business reorganization.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain order and payment records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect our services. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure. Please do not send sensitive information through website forms unless we specifically request it through a secure process.

Your choices and requests

You can manage location and notification permissions in your device settings. You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs. Email contact@speedi.delivery from the address connected to your request; we may need to verify your identity before responding.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our practices or legal obligations change and will post the revised policy with a new effective date. Contact Speedi Delivery LLC at contact@speedi.delivery or 140 W Franklin St, Unit 160, Chapel Hill, NC 27514.
