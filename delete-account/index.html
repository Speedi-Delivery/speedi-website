Delete your Speedi account

For Speedi and Speedi Drivers on iOS and Android, open the app → Profile → Delete Account → Delete.

An open order must be delivered or cancelled before your account can be deleted. Referral credit is forfeited; account deletion does not issue refunds.

If you cannot access the app, email contact@speedi.delivery from your account email to request deletion. You can use these instructions at speedi.delivery/delete-account to request account deletion without app access.

Speedi deletes your account and removes personal data as described in our Privacy Policy and applicable law. Some records may be retained where required or permitted by law. For help, contact contact@speedi.delivery.
