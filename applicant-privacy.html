Applicants

Applicant privacy notice.

How we use information submitted through the Work at Speedi form.

Effective date: September 22, 2026

What we collect

Our application form asks for your name, email address, phone number, desired role, transportation method, and any information you voluntarily include in the notes field. We also receive information included in any follow-up correspondence.

Do not submit a Social Security number, bank information, payment-card details, a photo of an ID, medical information, or other highly sensitive information through this website form. If we need information for an employment or contractor onboarding step, we will request it through an appropriate process.

How we use it

We use applicant information to review interest in working with Speedi, communicate about an application, assess role and availability fit, keep reasonable recruiting records, and meet legal obligations.

How it is handled

Website applications are submitted through FormSubmit to our designated email inbox. We may share applicant information with people who need it to evaluate or administer recruiting, and with service providers who help us operate the process. We do not sell applicant information.

Retention and requests

We retain applicant information for only as long as reasonably necessary for recruiting, recordkeeping, and legal requirements. You can request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted by emailing contact@speedi.delivery. We may need to verify your identity and may retain limited information where law permits or requires it.

Contact

For a privacy question about an application, email contact@speedi.delivery.
