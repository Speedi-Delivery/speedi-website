Help

Support & refunds.

The fastest way to help us find and resolve an order issue.

Need help with an order?

Contact us as soon as possible at contact@speedi.delivery. Include the email or phone number connected to the order, the order number, the approximate order time, and a short description of what happened. Do not email payment-card details or government-ID information.

Refunds, cancellations, and substitutions

We review issues individually based on the order details and the policies presented in the app or at checkout. Whether an order can be changed or cancelled depends on its stage of preparation and delivery. Any approved adjustment is returned through the original payment method or handled as agreed with support.

Website content does not change the prices, fees, availability, age-verification requirements, or terms displayed when an order is placed.

Accessibility and privacy requests

For help accessing the website, read our Accessibility Statement. For personal-information requests, read the Privacy Policy.

Contact

Email contact@speedi.delivery. Please do not use support email for emergencies. If there is an immediate safety emergency, contact local emergency services.
